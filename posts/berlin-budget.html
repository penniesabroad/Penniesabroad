2 Days in Berlin on a Budget: 3 Places Worth the Stop

New Pennies Abroad · Oct 13, 2026

This past weekend, I stayed in the city centre of Berlin and got two short days of German culture without breaking the bank. Think coffee for €3 and pasta for €6.90. Here are three places that are a must-stop on a student budget.

Note: All prices are from my trip on the weekend of October 10–11, 2026. Check the latest prices before you go.

1. Heart of Gold Hostel (€70 for two nights)

Johannisstraße 11, 10117 Berlin, Germany

I paid €70 total for two nights after getting my deposits for the room and padlock back. I stayed in one of the hostel's pods, and it was extremely convenient and gave me a lot of privacy. The pod locks completely, and it's big enough to fit two people, with enough bedding for both.

Each pod has adjustable lighting, chargers and a mirror. There are indoor and outdoor places to hang out, hot showers, and a shared kitchen with fridges where you can cook and keep leftovers. The staff were very friendly!

Tip: You can lock your things in the outside lockers for a €10 deposit, which you get back at the end of your stay. Breakfast is €10 a day. I skipped it and saved money by using the hostel kitchen.

2. Life Among People (€1.50–3 per coffee)

22 locations around Berlin

The coffee here was incredible. The shops are simple, but the coffee is amazing and the prices are very reasonable: €1.50 for an espresso, €2.50 for a cappuccino and €3 for a latte. Making your drink decaf or iced, or adding non-dairy milk, is free.

It was common to see people in Berlin walking around with Life Among People cups. If I were still in Germany, this is where I'd get my morning coffee every day.

3. La Cantina (€6.90 lunch menu)

Torstraße 124, 10119 Berlin, Germany

Between 11 AM and 2 PM, anything on La Cantina's lunch menu is €6.90. That covers everything from a personal pizza to a solid serving of carbonara. The restaurant is beautiful, with lots of natural light and cute Italian vibes.

Tip: Plan your day so you get there before 2 PM. Lunch is the cheapest way to eat a full sit-down meal here.

Final Thoughts

Two days is short, but Berlin is easy to do well on a student budget. A cheap, comfortable bed, €3 coffee and a €6.90 lunch left more of my budget for exploring the city. You don't need a big budget to have a great weekend in Berlin.
